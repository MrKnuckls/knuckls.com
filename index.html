Who I am

What's good — I'm Shaun (MrKnuckls), a PC gamer based in Dayton, Ohio. I spend my time deep in FiveM, DayZ, GTA, and Arc Raiders — and when I'm not playing, I'm hosting the servers we actually play on.

Everything runs out of my home lab, which is half battle-station, half science project. Proxmox, Docker, pfSense — the rack that keeps the servers online and the status feed green.

I'm also building SanRoque, a co-op open-world game in Unreal Engine 5. It's early days, but the vision is clear: a living world where you and your squad write the story.

This site is where you find my servers, what I'm into right now, and what's humming in the rack.

The home lab

Half battle-station, half science project. This rack powers everything.

Proxmox virtualization host running five VMs — game servers, Docker host, pfSense firewall, and a NAS for backups. The whole setup pulls about 200W and sits in my office closet.

  • ✓ Proxmox VE 9.2 — main hypervisor
  • ✓ Docker Swarm — game server containers
  • ✓ pfSense — VLANs, firewall, Pi-hole DNS
  • ✓ Status server — live green/red dot feed
  • ✓ Game servers — FiveM, DayZ, more
  • ✓ UPS backup — 30 min runtime on outage
